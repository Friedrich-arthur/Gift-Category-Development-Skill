# Repository work

This repository develops one portable overseas gift business-judgment skill.

- Keep one runtime identity, `gift-development-service`, and one `SKILL.md`. The installable directory contains only its five text files, including the on-demand `references/decision.md`.
- Clarify the business purpose, purchase/giving relationship and target consumer. Define requirements, identify resources and match them before giving a conditional gift-business decision. Reuse known information and ask only one decisive question at a time. Thinking steps are not a mandatory six-round interview.
- Ground directions in the buyer, giver, recipient, occasion, chosen market and available capabilities. Distinguish evidence, hypothesis, unknowns and recommendations.
- Recalculate timing and contribution when requirements or resources change. Keep dependencies, units, unknown lead times, purchase windows and receiving deadlines explicit. A component fitting a limit does not prove the whole gift fits.
- Do not turn interest, sample confirmation or existing capability into accepted trading terms, batch readiness or verified quality. Do not invent market demand, cultural practices, certification or commercial outcomes.
- Sampling execution, ordering, sales quotations, customer replies, purchasing and after-sales service are out of scope. Resource and cost analysis may constrain a judgment without becoming an instruction to execute.
- Keep the installed skill self-contained. No runtime downloads, credentials, hooks, extra skills, model changes or permission changes. Author service tips are optional and must not bias, gate or repeat throughout the consultation.
- Keep the same business instructions for WorkBuddy, Doubao Work and Codex. Never claim marketplace availability or native validation from file checks or a substitute model.
- Keep current, replayable cases and rubrics in `evals/`. Keep historical transcripts, screenshots, revision logs, internal tickets and generated archives outside the public source tree. Summarize current validation honestly in `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.
- Installation documentation is authored in `docs/INSTALLATION.md`. Regenerate `docs/site/install.html` with `tools/build_install_guide.py`; do not edit its generated copy independently.
- Run the maintenance tests, source/package checks and installation-page synchronization check for relevant changes. Business regression needs actual multi-turn answers and an independent verdict. Preserve original prompts when reproducing a defect.
- Do not add a license or legal claim without an explicit owner decision.
